3. "Same-host ads" — what that means (your question)

Think of youtube.com as one apartment building:

  • Different-building ads (DoubleClick, Innovid…): our firewall blocks the whole building. Video loads, ad never downloads. ✅
  • Same-building ads: YouTube serves the ad video itself from youtube.com / googlevideo.com — the SAME building the real video lives in. Blocking the building kills your video too, so we can't firewall it. Instead: hide its frame (CSS), click Skip (watchdog), or mute + jump past it (seek). Your 2 back-to-back unskippable ads were these — that's why no Skip button existed to click.
